About the HTTYD Quiz

Welcome to the ultimate How to Train Your Dragon (HTTYD) guessing quiz! This quiz is designed to test your knowledge of the incredible world of dragons from the beloved DreamWorks franchise.

How to Play

Your goal is to accurately guess as many dragon species as possible within the allotted time. As you type, the system instantly checks against our database covering dragons from the original films, spin-offs (like Riders of Berk and Race to the Edge), and the Hidden World.
